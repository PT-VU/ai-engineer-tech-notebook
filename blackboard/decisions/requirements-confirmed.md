## Confirmed product direction

- Primary job: help hiring-oriented readers quickly understand the author's AI Engineer focus, engineering judgment, and evidence.
- Primary audience: recruiters, hiring managers, and AI engineering interviewers; technical peers are a secondary audience.
- Information architecture: hybrid portfolio + notebook.
- MVP navigation: Home, Projects, Writing, About / CV.
- Publication boundary: curated, explicit, owner-reviewed publication only; never mirror F:\ automatically.
- Source of truth: curated Markdown in the project repository.
- Deployment baseline: public static site with no required server runtime, using a free-first provider selected from the landscape report.
- MVP excludes: login, comments, newsletter infrastructure, dynamic AI chat, database, and automatic F:\ ingestion.
- Review Agent template: `基础AI工程师角色`.

## Language and editorial direction

- Technical articles are primarily Chinese.
- Every technical article must display a Chinese title, an English title, and an English summary near the top.
- The site should foreground the author's personal reasoning, judgment, trade-offs, preferences, and changing views.
- External sources and framework documentation support the author's argument; the site should not read like a neutral literature dump.
- Project pages may use a bilingual surface where helpful for CV readers, but the editorial voice remains personal and evidence-led.

## Authority and acceptance intent

Architecture may choose the implementation details needed to satisfy these requirements, but may not silently change the audience, language policy, publication boundary, or evidence-first editorial direction.
